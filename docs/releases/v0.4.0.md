Fourth pre-release of BlindVault: vaults and backups made with a 0.x version may not open in a later version, and no migration is planned for them (D177). It brings Phase 5 (synchronization) for the manual measurement of a same-network sync where both devices send only mDNS names (D161, D212).

What changed since v0.3.0:

- sync: from Settings › Devices, two devices of the same vault sync directly over WebRTC. The user picks the other device on both, one shows its code and the other reads it, by QR code or copy/paste, then the other way. The codes are encrypted to the picked device, so the channel that carries them sees only an opaque text. Both devices send what the other lacks or holds older, and each item keeps the latest edit, with the other one in its history;
- live sync: once both devices have committed, each edit reaches the other device without a new exchange, until End, a lock or another flow. A bar in the vault shows it;
- sync state: the other device's row says "Syncing now" or "Live sync", every last sync shows its date and time, and This device shows the latest sync with any device;
- the trash, its automatic purge, the history of each item, retirements and a regenerated Recovery Phrase reach the other device in one sync;
- a device clock gone back: the vault stays read-only until the date and time it names, and still syncs;
- records in a newer format from another device are rejected and named in the backup warning until a sync without them, or until the user dismisses the device.

The service worker is unchanged since v0.1.0.
