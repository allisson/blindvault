First release of BlindVault, a pre-release: vaults and backups made with a 0.x version may not open in a later version, and no migration is planned for them (D177).

What it holds, in short:

- one vault per installation, locked by a local password, with WebAuthn PRF unlock where the browser supports it;
- items from templates, folders, the trash and item history, search in memory;
- encrypted `.bvault` backups, restore and merge, the Recovery Phrase, and the forgotten-password reset;
- the installable PWA: a verified, versioned cache, updates only after confirmation, a warning when the service worker changes, and the About screen with the asset manifest hash.

There is no sync between devices yet.

Served at https://blindvault.allisson.dev.
