Tenth pre-release of BlindVault: vaults and backups made with a 0.x version may not open in a later version, and no migration is planned for them (D177). It closes the Essential UX Gate (D244) with the fixes from the audit of the Phase 7b screens in a real browser (D256), before Phase 8. Before you use it, read [docs/limitations.md](../limitations.md).

What changed since v0.7.2, presentation only (D256):

- a shown seed phrase takes the whole row, with Hide and Copy below it, and no word breaks across lines on a phone or a tablet;
- the strength meter's empty bars use the TOTP ring's track colour and show on the light theme.

The service worker is unchanged since v0.1.0.

Served at https://blindvault.allisson.dev.
