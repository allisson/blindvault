Sixth pre-release of BlindVault: vaults and backups made with a 0.x version may not open in a later version, and no migration is planned for them (D177). It brings Phase 7 (user experience) and the import of Phase 8, for the manual measurement of LAN-only pairing and sync where both devices send only mDNS names (D161, D212). Before you use it, read [docs/limitations.md](../limitations.md).

What changed since v0.5.0:

- appearance: a new visual identity, with a light and a dark theme that follow the system by default and six accent colors, chosen in Settings › Appearance (D231, D232, D233);
- flows: Vault ready opens the pairing or the re-admission already started, a finished live sync keeps running when its panel closes, and leaving the item editor with unsaved changes asks first (D234);
- copy: the English and Brazilian Portuguese text follows the glossary (D235);
- motion: dialogs enter, pressed buttons respond, and a check confirms each copy; nothing moves under reduced motion, and no warning ever animates (D236);
- accessibility: reviewed against WCAG 2.2 AA, with errors read again on focus, masked secrets read as "Hidden", and every screen checked by axe, by the keyboard alone and by its accessibility tree in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (D237, D238, D240, D241);
- layout: a long list scrolls in its own pane, with the header and Lock kept in view (D239);
- import: from Settings, the items of a BlindPass export (`.json` or encrypted `.blindpass`), Chrome, LastPass, Apple Passwords, KeePassXC, Bitwarden, Proton Pass, 1Password and Dashlane go into a new folder, after a preview and in one commit. The file is read only in memory and must not exceed 100 MB, and an encrypted Bitwarden or Proton Pass export is refused with a message to export unencrypted (D123 to D126, D130, D146, D242);
- dependencies: vite, vitest and source-map-js updated past their advisories.

The service worker is unchanged since v0.1.0.

Served at https://blindvault.allisson.dev.
