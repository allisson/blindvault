Ninth pre-release of BlindVault: vaults and backups made with a 0.x version may not open in a later version, and no migration is planned for them (D177). It follows a capture of every v0.7.1 screen and an HTML prototype of the layout and presentation changes (D254). It is for another hands-on test before the Security Gate of Phase 8 (D244). Before you use it, read [docs/limitations.md](../limitations.md).

What changed since v0.7.1, presentation only: no step, check, warning, confirmation or security rule is removed, and every warning keeps its text (D254):

- notices: the preview banner becomes a "Preview" badge beside the app's name, the storage warning is one line with "Details", the backup reminder sits at the top of the item list, and the lock badge is neutral;
- lists: folders and types are pills, opening a bottom sheet on a phone and a popover elsewhere; the toast sits above the phone's bottom navigation;
- item detail and editor: a bar with Edit and the "…" menu, divided field rows, icon buttons on non-secret fields, Cancel and Save in a fixed bar, and the generator in a dialog or a bottom sheet;
- every password field has "Show password", masked again on submit, on a screen change and on lock;
- Settings opens inside the vault frame on tablet and desktop; Devices offers "Pair a device" and "Re-admit a device" as two cards;
- wizards open on a screen of their own, with "Next:", Cancel in the header or beside the main action, the main action fixed at the bottom on a phone, and a drop area for files; a live sync's last step stays below the device list (D255);
- type and spacing: 22 px screen titles, 11 px section labels, 15 px field values, spacing in multiples of 4 px.

The service worker is unchanged since v0.1.0.
